# KPI Tree

## Business Outcome: Faster Partner-Support Resolution

- Operational KPI: triage time proxy and escalation precision.
- Agent metric: orchestrator routing accuracy and required-tool use.
- Safety metric: consent-boundary adherence and unsupported-claim rate.

## Business Outcome: Safer Customer Communication

- Operational KPI: reviewer correction rate and human approval queue health.
- Agent metric: evaluator catch rate and prohibited-action avoidance.
- Safety metric: unsupported action rate and escalation recall.

## Business Outcome: Evidence-Backed Pilot Decision

- Operational KPI: launch recommendation quality and blocker clarity.
- Agent metric: regression pass rate and risk-weighted score.
- Safety metric: redaction coverage and public-safe evidence completeness.
